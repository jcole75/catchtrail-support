Privacy policy

Effective August 23, 2026

CatchTrail has no account, advertising, analytics, social feed, or developer-operated cloud service. Your fishing journal is stored in the app on your device.

Information stored on your device

Journal entries, coordinates, notes, measurements, settings, and photos are stored in CatchTrail’s app-private storage. CatchTrail does not automatically upload or synchronize that journal to the developer.

CatchTrail creates an export only when you choose Create a backup. The resulting JSON file is controlled by you and can include embedded photos and precise fishing locations. Store and share backup files carefully.

Optional location and network requests

  • Location and weather: CatchTrail requests foreground location only after you tap Use current location. It asks the operating system for a place name and sends the selected latitude and longitude over HTTPS to Open-Meteo to retrieve current conditions. The request is not tied to a CatchTrail account because no account exists.
  • Maps: Viewing a map requests map data from the platform map provider. The web version uses OpenStreetMap. Those providers may receive technical request information under their own privacy terms.
  • Distribution: Google Play and the device operating system have their own distribution, security, and optional system-backup behavior. They are not CatchTrail journal-synchronization services.

Permissions

  • Camera: take a catch photo when you request it
  • Photo library: attach an existing photo when you request it
  • Foreground location: record the current fishing location when you request it

CatchTrail does not request background location or microphone access.

Deleting your data

Delete individual journal entries inside CatchTrail. To remove all local app data, clear CatchTrail’s storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. CatchTrail cannot delete exported backup files for you; remove those from whichever destination you selected.

Children

CatchTrail is a general-audience utility and is not directed to children. It does not knowingly collect personal information through a developer server.

Changes and support

This policy will be updated before any release that materially changes CatchTrail’s data practices. For privacy questions or support, open a request at CatchTrail Support.
