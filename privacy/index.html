Privacy policy

Effective September 1, 2026

CatchTrail has no app account, social feed, or developer-operated journal cloud. Your fishing journal is stored in the app on your device. Free users may see consent-managed Google AdMob banners; Plus and Play Pass access remove them.

Information stored on your device

Journal entries, coordinates, notes, measurements, settings, and photos are stored in CatchTrail’s app-private storage. CatchTrail does not automatically upload or synchronize that journal to the developer.

CatchTrail creates an export only when you choose Create a backup. The resulting JSON file is controlled by you and can include embedded photos and precise fishing locations. Store and share backup files carefully.

Optional location and network requests

  • Location and weather: CatchTrail requests foreground location only after you choose automatic location during setup or tap Use current location. It asks the operating system for a place name and sends the selected latitude and longitude over HTTPS to Open-Meteo to retrieve current conditions. The request is not tied to a CatchTrail account because no account exists.
  • Nearby fish: When you tap Near me, CatchTrail sends the selected latitude and longitude to iNaturalist over HTTPS to retrieve counts of research-grade fish observations near that point. It starts with a 10 km radius and widens to at most 50 km only when the closer search has too few useful choices. CatchTrail does not send journal entries, photos, notes, measurements, or an account identifier to iNaturalist.
  • Maps: Opening the native Map tab requests map styles, vector tiles, fonts, and icons from OpenFreeMap. Those requests contain the map-tile coordinates needed to display the area around saved catches and normal technical information such as the device IP address and user agent. The map data is derived from OpenStreetMap. The web version loads an OpenStreetMap embed for the selected coordinates. CatchTrail does not send journal notes, photos, measurements, or a CatchTrail account identifier to either map service.
  • Distribution: Google Play and the device operating system have their own distribution, security, and optional system-backup behavior. They are not CatchTrail journal-synchronization services.
  • Advertising: The free version can show a restrained banner supplied by Google AdMob. CatchTrail requests legally required consent before requesting ads and exposes ad privacy choices in Settings when required. Google’s Mobile Ads SDK can collect and share IP address, app interactions, diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Google documents encryption in transit. CatchTrail does not put journal entries, photos, notes, measurements, or saved fishing coordinates in ad requests. Plus entitlement holders receive no ads.
  • Purchases: The applicable app store processes payment details. RevenueCat validates CatchTrail Plus using an anonymous app user ID and receives store purchase history, product identifiers, and entitlement status. CatchTrail disables automatic advertising-device- identifier collection in RevenueCat and does not send contact details or journal content to RevenueCat.

CatchTrail Plus and Play Pass

CatchTrail Plus is an auto-renewing annual subscription that removes advertising. The app checks entitlement status when it opens and when it returns to the foreground. If CatchTrail is accepted into Google Play Pass, a separate Play Pass in-app product unlocks the same Plus entitlement. Both access paths are treated as ad-free.

Purchases can be restored from Settings. The store provides subscription cancellation and management. CatchTrail does not currently provide an app account that transfers access between unrelated Apple and Google store accounts.

Permissions

  • Camera: take a catch photo when you request it
  • Photo library: attach an existing photo when you request it
  • Foreground location: record the current fishing location when you request it

CatchTrail does not request background location or microphone access.

Deleting your data

Delete individual journal entries inside CatchTrail. To remove all local app data, clear CatchTrail’s storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. CatchTrail cannot delete exported backup files for you; remove those from whichever destination you selected.

Children

CatchTrail is a general-audience utility and is not directed to children. It does not knowingly collect personal information through a developer server.

Changes and support

This policy will be updated before any release that materially changes CatchTrail’s data practices. For privacy questions or support, open a email support@mindware.mobi.
