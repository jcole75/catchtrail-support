CatchTrail Plus terms

Effective September 1, 2026

CatchTrail Plus costs $9.95 per year in the United States, renews automatically until canceled, and removes advertising from CatchTrail.

What Plus includes

An active CatchTrail Plus entitlement removes banner advertising. The core fishing journal remains fully usable with ads without Plus.

Billing and renewal

The applicable app store charges the price shown in its purchase sheet. Prices can vary by country, tax, and currency. Unless canceled through the store before renewal, an annual subscription renews for another year at the then-displayed price. CatchTrail does not receive full payment-card details.

Cancellation, restore, and refunds

Manage or cancel through the store account used to buy Plus. Cancellation stops future renewal and normally leaves Plus active through the paid period. Use Restore purchases in CatchTrail Settings when access is missing. Refunds follow the applicable store’s policy.

Google Play Pass

If CatchTrail is accepted into Google Play Pass, Google can unlock the same ad-free Plus entitlement through a separate Play Pass product. Play Pass availability and membership are governed by Google’s terms and can change independently of the paid annual subscription.

Support

Questions? Email support@mindware.mobi. See the CatchTrail privacy policy for data practices.
